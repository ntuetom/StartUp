常見問題

可以免費玩嗎？

每部互動電影都可以免費玩到第一個關鍵抉擇。之後如果想繼續，可以一次性解鎖該部電影，也可以兩部一起解鎖。解鎖是永久的，不是訂閱，之後也不會再有其他收費。

換了手機或重新安裝，怎麼恢復購買？

請用購買時的同一個 Apple ID 登入 App Store，然後在 App 首頁打開「遊戲設定」，點「恢復購買」。在解鎖畫面上也可以找到「恢復購買」按鈕。

遊戲進度會跟著換到新手機嗎？

不會。為了保護你的隱私，遊戲進度、已解鎖的結局和典藏紀錄都只存在目前這台裝置上，不會上傳或同步。刪除 App 後，這些紀錄也會一起消失。已購買的電影可以隨時恢復。

家人可以一起使用我買的電影嗎？

可以。電影解鎖支援「家人共享」。只要你已在 iOS 設定中開啟家人共享，家庭成員就能用他們自己的 Apple ID 恢復購買。

怎麼看到其他結局？

每部電影都有 8 種結局。到首頁的「典藏」查看結局，分支地圖會顯示你走過的路線。在已解鎖的電影中，點一個你走過的抉擇，就能從那裡重新選擇。遊玩途中也可以從畫面右上角回到上一個抉擇。

怎麼切換中文／英文？

在首頁打開「遊戲設定」，從「語言」選擇。

購買失敗或被重複扣款怎麼辦？

App Store 的付款與退款都由 Apple 處理。請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請，或來信告訴我，我會協助你確認。

FAQ

Is it free to play?

Each interactive film is free up to its first major decision. To keep going, you can unlock that film with a one-time purchase, or unlock both films together. Unlocks are permanent: there is no subscription and nothing else to pay for afterward.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy, then open Settings from the app's home screen and tap Restore Purchases. The unlock screen also has a Restore Purchases button.

Does my progress carry over to a new device?

No. To protect your privacy, story progress, unlocked endings, and your collection are stored only on the current device and are never uploaded or synced. Deleting the app also deletes them. Purchased films can always be restored.

Can my family use the films I bought?

Yes. Film unlocks support Family Sharing. If Family Sharing is turned on in iOS Settings, family members can restore the purchase with their own Apple ID.

How do I see other endings?

Each film has eight endings. Open Collection on the home screen to view your endings, along with a branch map of the routes you have taken. In a film you have unlocked, tap a decision you have already reached to replay from that point. During play, you can also go back to the last decision from the top-right corner of the screen.

How do I switch between English and Chinese?

Open Settings from the home screen and choose under Language.

A purchase failed or I was charged twice. What should I do?

App Store payments and refunds are handled by Apple. Please request help at reportaproblem.apple.com, or email me and I'll help you look into it.
