你的選擇，就是劇本

Your choices are the script

8

每部 8 種結局

三個關鍵抉擇，分出真愛、救贖、背叛到宿敵的不同結局。

Eight endings per film. Three key decisions split the story from true love and redemption to betrayal and rivalry.

♡

看不見的信任

對方對你的信任在暗中累積。有些結局，只有真正走進對方心裡才看得到。

Trust builds out of sight. Some endings open only if you have truly earned it.

⟲

回到抉擇的那一刻

分支地圖記下你走過的路。回到任何一個走過的抉擇，選另一條路。

A branch map records the routes you have taken. Return to any decision you have reached and choose differently.

◐

典藏與花絮

收集場景、角色表情和結局。有些結局之後，還藏著花絮。還能讓角色站進收集到的任何場景，存成照片或分享給朋友。

Collect scenes, expressions, and endings. Some endings hide an epilogue. Stand a character in any scene you have collected, then save or share the picture.
