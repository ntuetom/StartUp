隱私權政策

本政策適用於 iOS App「啟程」（英文名稱 StartUp，以下稱「本 App」）。本 App 由個人開發者 Hungyi Wu（以下稱「我」）開發與營運。

一句話說明：本 App 不蒐集、不傳送、也不分享你的任何個人資料。

不蒐集的資料

  • 不需要註冊帳號或登入，也不會要求你的姓名、Email、電話等資訊。
  • 沒有廣告，沒有任何追蹤或分析工具，也沒有使用任何第三方 SDK。
  • 不會讀取你的位置、聯絡人、照片、相機或麥克風。
  • 遊戲進行時不連上任何伺服器，你的遊玩紀錄不會離開你的裝置。

只存在你裝置上的資料

為了讓你能接續遊玩，本 App 會在你的裝置本機儲存以下內容：遊戲進度、已解鎖的結局與典藏內容、已讀紀錄，以及語言、音效、震動等設定。

這些資料不會上傳給我或任何第三方，也不會透過 iCloud 同步。刪除本 App 時，這些資料會一併從裝置上移除。

購買

本 App 的付費內容（電影解鎖）由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。付款資訊由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理，我不會取得或儲存你的信用卡、付款或 Apple ID 資訊。本 App 只會向 App Store 確認你已擁有哪些購買項目，以便解鎖內容與恢復購買。

Apple 提供的診斷資料

如果你在 iOS 的「設定」中選擇與 App 開發者分享分析資料，Apple 可能會提供我匿名、彙總的當機報告與使用統計。這些資料無法識別你的身分，是否分享完全由你在 iOS 設定中決定，本 App 本身不會另外蒐集。

兒童隱私

本 App 不會蒐集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。

政策更新

如果本 App 日後的資料處理方式有所改變（例如新增需要連網的功能），我會在 App 推出該更新之前修訂本頁，並更新下方的生效日期。

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the iOS app StartUp (Traditional Chinese name: 啟程; "the App"). The App is developed and operated by Hungyi Wu, an individual developer ("I", "me").

In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

Data I Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in is required, and the App never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or similar information.
  • The App contains no advertising, no tracking or analytics tools, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The App does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • The App does not connect to any server during play. Your play history never leaves your device.

Data Stored Only on Your Device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the App stores the following locally on your device: story progress, unlocked endings and collection items, read history, and settings such as language, sound, and haptics.

This data is never uploaded to me or to any third party, and it is not synced through iCloud. Deleting the App removes this data from your device.

Purchases

Paid content in the App (film unlocks) is handled by Apple's App Store. Payment information is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; I never receive or store your credit card, payment, or Apple ID details. The App only asks the App Store which purchases you own, so it can unlock content and restore purchases.

Diagnostics Provided by Apple

If you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide me with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. This information cannot identify you, sharing is entirely your choice in iOS Settings, and the App itself collects nothing on top of it.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If the way the App handles data ever changes (for example, if a feature that needs a network connection is added), I will revise this page before that update is released and change the effective date below.
